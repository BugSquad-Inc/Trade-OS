

# BUTLER’S LEATHER CHENNAI — TRADE OS PROTOTYPE SPECIFICATION & 15-MINUTE SALES DEMO PLAN

## Executive Positioning

**Trade OS is not a buyer directory.**  
It is a **buyer acquisition, compliance, and export pipeline operating system** for Butler’s Leather Chennai.

The prototype must make the CEO / Export Director think:

> “This is not a list. This is a ranked export attack plan with real European buyers, real compliance pressure, real freight economics, and ready-to-send outreach.”

The demo should sell one outcome:

> **5 qualified European buyer matches in 14 days, with compliance-ready pitch assets, for $500.**

If those 5 matches are genuinely qualified, the $500 pilot becomes the easiest yes of the year.

---

# IMPORTANT DEMO INTEGRITY RULE

Because this is a live sales prototype for a real company, do **not** fake “live” data.

Use this rule:

> **Every buyer, signal, contact title, compliance claim, freight rate, and price benchmark shown in the demo must be either:**
>
> 1. Verified from a public source with URL / screenshot / date,  
> 2. Sourced from your licensed B2B dataset, or  
> 3. Clearly labeled as “Sample / Illustrative” until connected to live data.

For the demo, you can say:

> “This is a working prototype. Some fields are illustrative until we connect your live catalog, certificates, and preferred freight forwarder. The buyer logic, compliance mapping, and outreach workflow are fully functional.”

That preserves trust while still making the product feel powerful.

### 6. Matching Engine Hard Filters

Do not match if:

- Buyer requires a leather species Butler’s does not produce.
- Buyer requires a certification Butler’s cannot provide and cannot close within 30 days.
- Buyer requires EUDR full traceability and Butler’s has no path to provide it.
- Buyer’s minimum volume is below Butler’s economic MOQ.
- Buyer is in a sanctioned country or restricted entity.
- Buyer requires a finish / article outside Butler’s capability.
