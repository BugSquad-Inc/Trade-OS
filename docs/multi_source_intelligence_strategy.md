## 1. 📊 The 6 Core Business Intelligence Streams

To make Trade OS an indispensable commercial product for clients like **Butler's Leather**, we aggregate and cross-synthesize **6 Business Data Streams**:

```
                                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                                  │      6 BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE STREAMS         │
                                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                                         │

       ▼                  ▼                  ▼                  ▼                  ▼                  ▼

   Specs Directory      & Expos (Exhibitors) News & Media       Policy Watch       LinkedIn Signals   Index (Hides/Chem)
```

### Stream 1: Web Catalog & Specifications Directory
- **Commercial Value**: Hard data on supplier capabilities, MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity), leather types (Wet Blue, Crust, Nappa, Calfskin), and contact info.
- **Client Use Case**: Instant supplier filtering for procurement managers.

### Stream 2: Live Trade Shows & Expo Intelligence (Lineapelle, ACLE, APLF, ILM)
- **Data Harvested**: Exhibitor directories, booth locations, newly unveiled seasonal collections, featured tanning innovations.
- **Commercial Value**: Tells buyers *who* is actively exhibiting, *what* new textures/colors are launched for Autumn/Winter 2026/2027, and *which* competitors are present.
- **Client Use Case**: Butler's Leather sees which German tanneries are exhibiting at Lineapelle Milan without spending $5,000 on travel.

### Stream 3: Daily Industry News & Trade Media
- **Data Harvested**: *World Leather*, *Leather International*, *Sourcing Journal*, *La Conceria*, *Fibre2Fashion*.
- **Commercial Value**: Tracks company mergers, factory expansions, insolvency filings, and supply disruptions in real time.
- **Client Use Case**: Alert sent when a competitor's main tannery faces supply delays, creating an instant opening for Butler's Leather to step in.

### Stream 4: Regulatory, Environmental & Policy Watch (EUDR, REACH, EN 18199)
- **Data Harvested**: European Commission directives, EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) scope updates, REACH chemical bans (PFAS, Chromium VI restrictions).
- **Commercial Value**: Compliance intelligence. Ensures buyers avoid non-compliant tanneries subject to EU fines.
- **Client Use Case**: Reassures luxury brand buyers that Butler's Leather suppliers meet European eco-traceability standards (**EN 18199**).

### Stream 5: Social Intent, LinkedIn & Industry Forum Signals
- **Data Harvested**: LinkedIn job postings (*"Hiring Head of Leather Procurement at Hugo Boss"*), company updates, Reddit (`r/leathercraft`, `r/procurement`), PurseBlog sentiment.
- **Commercial Value**: **Hiring Signals = Active Buying Intent**. When a brand hires a new Leather Purchasing Manager, they re-evaluate their supplier list within 90 days.
- **Client Use Case**: Butler's Leather gets notified of high-priority procurement hiring events to pitch early.

### Stream 6: Raw Material & Commodity Price Index
- **Data Harvested**: US Heavy Native Steers (HNS), European hide index, synthetic solvent tariffs.
- **Commercial Value**: Empowers buyers to negotiate hide prices based on real-time commodity cost trends.
