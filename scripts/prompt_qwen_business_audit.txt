# STRATEGIC CPO & B2B COMMERCIAL AUDIT REQUEST FOR TRADE OS

## CONTEXT & CURRENT STATE OF THE MVP
Trade OS has completed all 7 technical sprints across its 11 modules for the **Leather & Materials Vertical Wedge** (Target: Indian leather exporters like Butler's Leather Chennai selling to European buyers in Germany, Italy, France, Spain, UK).

### What Has Been Built:
1. **Medallion Architecture (PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector on Docker)**:
   - Bronze raw ingestion (6 intelligence streams: customs, tenders, regulatory, trade directories, company registries, port movements).
   - Silver normalized entities (`entity_company`, `entity_person`, `customs_shipments_normalized`).
   - Gold decision tables (`match_candidate`, `signal`, `exporter_capability`, `agent_runs`, `crm_export_log`, `webhook_subscriptions`).
2. **Scoring Engine**:
   - Canonical 100-point explainable formula: 35 Product Fit + 25 Compliance Readiness (EUDR 68/100, REACH) + 15 Lane Economics (INMAA->DEHAM transit & freight) + 15 Buyer Intent + 10 Accessibility.
   - 100% explainability coverage (every match has 5 positive/negative ScoreDriver badges, zero zero-driver matches).
3. **Hybrid Search & AI Multi-Agents**:
   - Dense vector (pgvector HNSW) + Sparse text (BM25 tsvector) combined via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF k=60).
   - LangGraph 5-Agent Swarm (ResearchAgent, ComplianceAgent, NarrativeAgent, OutreachSequenceAgent, AccountPlanAgent) with human approval gate.
4. **Customs & Commercial Hub**:
   - Ocean BOL manifests (TEU volume, container flows, port pairs).
   - 1-Click CRM export to HubSpot, Salesforce, and CSV.
   - Real-time WebSockets (`/ws/signals`) and HMAC-SHA256 webhooks.
5. **Frontend UI (Apple HIG Silver-White Theme)**:
   - Screen 1: Match Portal (50+ European buyers, Grade A/B pills, MatchCard, ScoreRing).
   - Screen 2: Live Signals Feed, EUDR 68/100 scorecard, Multi-Corridor Ocean Matrix (Hamburg, Genoa, Le Havre, Valencia).
   - Screen 3: Account 360 buyer dossier, 4-tone AI Outreach Composer, LangGraph Cockpit.
   - Screen 4: Customs Manifest Explorer.
   - Screen 5: Executive KPI Dashboard & Governance.

---

## THE CORE STRATEGIC & BUSINESS PROBLEM:
The user is concerned that **the MVP is currently heavily developer-friendly and engineering-focused rather than business-friendly and buyer/seller-friendly**. 

Specifically:
1. The terminology and presentation feel like an engineering console (e.g., "Medallion Data Sync", "RRF 0.032 Score", "LangGraph Agent Swarm", "TEU Manifests", "Incoterms Benchmark", "HMAC Webhooks") rather than a direct, intuitive **Export Revenue Engine** for a 50+ year old tannery owner or export director in Ambur/Chennai/Kanpur.
2. A non-technical Indian exporter doesn't care about the backend pipeline architecture; they care about:
   - *"Which German buyer will buy 20,000 sq ft of my shoe upper leather this month?"*
   - *"How much money will I make per container after freight and EUDR compliance?"*
   - *"What exact email should I send to the Head of Procurement right now to get a sample request?"*
   - *"Why is this buyer ranked #1 over #5, and can I trust this data before I contact them?"*
